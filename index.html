Welcome

The Inner Wheel Club of Dagupan East is a vibrant women’s service organization affiliated with the Inner Wheel Clubs of the Philippines, Inc. (IWCPI), District 379. Rooted in the ideals of friendship, personal service, and international understanding, our members work hand-in-hand with the Rotary Club of Dagupan East to uplift communities in Dagupan City and surrounding areas.

Through regular meetings, livelihood projects, health awareness programs, feeding initiatives, and partnerships with local and international organizations, we continue the Inner Wheel tradition of turning compassion into concrete action.

What We Do

🤝

Fellowship & Friendship

Regular club meetings foster strong bonds among members while strengthening collaboration with Rotary and other community partners.

❤️

Community Service

From feeding programs and livelihood training to health orientations and donations, we respond to real needs in Dagupan and nearby barangays.

🌍

International Understanding

As part of International Inner Wheel, we connect with clubs worldwide and support projects that promote peace and goodwill.
