Inner Wheel Club of Dagupan East District 379 July 17, 2026

🌸 Blooming in Abundance 🌸

The Inner Wheel Club of Dagupan East was honored to attend the 41st IWCPI District 379 Induction & Turnover Ceremonies held at the Awesome Hotel Ballroom, San Juan, La Union with the inspiring theme, “Blooming in Abundance.”

This meaningful gathering celebrated a new season of service, leadership, and sisterhood—bringing together Inner Wheel members from across the district to reaffirm our commitment to making a positive impact in our communities.

As we begin another Inner Wheel year, we look forward to growing together, serving with compassion, and creating lasting change through friendship and fellowship.

Congratulations to all the newly inducted District Officers, Club Officers, and members. Here’s to a fruitful year of service!

Together, we flourish. 🌸💙

Inner Wheel Club of Dagupan East District 379 July 16, 2026

The Inner Wheel Club of Dagupan East was honored to attend the 61st National Induction and Turnover Ceremonies of the Inner Wheel Clubs of the Philippines, Inc. held at the Grand Ballroom, Solaire Resort North.

This prestigious gathering celebrated a new chapter of leadership, fellowship, and service, inspiring us to continue making a positive impact in our communities. We are proud to stand alongside fellow Inner Wheel members as we reaffirm our commitment to the ideals of Friendship, Service, and International Understanding.

Together, we look forward to another meaningful year of serving with compassion and purpose.

Congratulations to all the National Officers and leaders of IWCPI!

A New Chapter Begins!

The Inner Wheel Club of Dagupan East proudly celebrates the successful Induction of Officers and New Members, held at Cabalen, Dagupan City.
